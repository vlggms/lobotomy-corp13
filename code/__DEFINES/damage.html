code/__DEFINES/damage.dm

DAMAGE_UNTRACKABLESet this bitflag to make the source of the damage untrackable (thus, mobs do not aggro onto the target from it, and PostDamageReaction will not have a source when it runs)
DAMAGE_NO_SPREADSet this bitflag to prevent damage from being spread across the body. Irrelevant for anything but carbons.
DAMAGE_WHITE_HEALABLESet this bitflag to allow WHITE/BLACK damage to resane.
DAMAGE_FORCEDSet this bitflag to ignore the armour check and godmode (yes, for some reason you can ignore godmode. do not blame this on me)
ATTACK_TYPE_MELEESet this bitflag to mark the attack as melee (EGO melee weapons, fisticuffs, an abnormality's basic attack...)
ATTACK_TYPE_RANGEDSet this bitflag to mark the attack as ranged (firearms, bows, etc...) or thrown attack (javelins, boomerangs, something flung by Sirocco...)
ATTACK_TYPE_SPECIALSet this bitflag to mark the attack as a special attack (Fragment of the Universe's song, Temperance Insanity pulse damage, EGO special abilities, most Abnormality unique attacks... Very broad definition)
ATTACK_TYPE_STATUSSet this bitflag to mark the attack as a status effect (Melting Love's DoT, damage from burning, etc...)
ATTACK_TYPE_ENVIRONMENTSet this bitflag to mark the attack as coming from the environment (stepping on a fire turf, stepping on a Hypocrisy trap, drowning in the Great Lakes, etc...)
ATTACK_TYPE_COUNTERSet this bitflag when the attack is a "counter" or "riposte". (White Fixer's damage reflection, Sanguine Flame's deflect...)
ATTACK_TYPE_OTHERThis is for when the damage is coming from somewhere that doesn't easily fit one of the other categories, but you'd still like to mark its type...
